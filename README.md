# angerona

Keeps your personal details out of what you publish with git: your emails,
your home path, your login name and your machine's name. Secrets are for
gitleaks and friends; angerona covers the details that point back at you,
such as a work email on a cherry-picked commit, a `Co-authored-by` trailer,
or `/Users/<login>/...` in an agent's `.mcp.json`.

Angerona was the Roman goddess shown with her mouth sealed, who kept the secret
name of Rome.

Not usable yet: being rebuilt on branch feat/keep-you-out.
